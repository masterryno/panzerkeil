About the game

In one line. Panzerkeil is a turn-based hex wargame of the Second World War for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Build an army of your own and lead it through 48 battles in three campaigns, then take it against other players in Battle Mode.

Panzerkeil is a turn-based hex wargame of the Second World War, made only for Apple devices: one app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Players choose a side and lead it through the war in three campaigns, German, Western Allied and Soviet, across 48 battles on hand-drawn operational maps, with 130 illustrated unit types from cavalry to jet fighters.

The army travels with the player from battle to battle. Units carry names, their own trading cards, service records, traits earned from what they have lived through, and medals from the Iron Cross to the Victoria Cross. How a battle is won decides where the war goes next, and every battle opens with a voiced briefing and closes with what really happened, to set the player's war beside the real one.

Battle Mode takes a veteran Battle Group of the player's own against other players in turn-based matches through Game Center, under fog of war. A Daily and a Weekly Operation give everyone the same battle, with leaderboards.

Panzerkeil is free to start. One purchase, The Whole War, unlocks every battle on every device. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no account, and every battle plays offline. It is out on 15 January 2027, and pre-orders are open on the App Store.

What is in it

Features

  • Three campaigns and 48 battles, with branching roads through the war
  • 130 illustrated unit types, from cavalry to jet fighters
  • An army that carries over: names, trading cards, traits, medals and service records
  • Battle Mode: your own Battle Group against other players, under fog of war
  • Turn-based matches through Game Center, with chat and rematches
  • Morale, encirclement, supply lines, sieges, airlifts and weather by unit type
  • Every attack shows its odds, with every modifier, before you commit
  • Voiced briefings, real histories and battlefield calls
  • Daily and Weekly Operations with leaderboards and battle films
  • 25 Game Center achievements and six leaderboards
  • Playable lessons, three difficulty levels, Dynamic Type and colour-vision settings
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; iCloud sync between devices
