Help

Questions and answers

Panzerkeil is a turn-based hex wargame for iPhone and iPad. If your question isn't answered here, write to us.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and suggestions: levihopper+panzerkeil@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and, for a bug, the battle and turn where it happened.

Playing

How do I start a different campaign?

On the title screen, tap the campaign you want (Axis, Western Allies or Red Army), then New Campaign or Continue Campaign. Each campaign keeps its own save. Quick Battle lets you fight any of the 48 battles on its own.

What do the difficulty levels change?

Easy: the enemy fields four-fifths of its strength and makes more mistakes. Normal: the battles as written. Hard: every enemy unit is a grade more experienced, the enemy plays carefully, and victories pay less prestige. The enemy commander's level also follows yours: about half on Easy, the same on Normal, ahead of you on Hard.

How do I see where a unit will go?

Select a unit, then touch a shaded hex: the route and what it costs appear while your finger is down. Lift to move there, or slide off the shaded ground to cancel. Before an attack, an arrow shows the target and the odds.

Why did my veteran unit drop a grade?

Refitting with free recruits brings in new men, which lowers a unit's experience. Refitting with veterans costs prestige but keeps the grade. Headquarters warns you before recruits lower a grade.

Your commander

What are postings?

While the game is closed, your commander works, up to a day at a time. On the Commander page choose Study historical battles or Study troop weakness to unlock chapters of the Field Manual, or Train troops for experience and to drill one Green unit up to Trained.

What does the Field Manual do?

Each chapter explains part of the game in depth (suppression, sieges, weather, supply and how to beat each kind of unit) and adds advice on the battlefield, such as siege odds in the attack preview or the best answer to an enemy unit when you touch and hold it.

Can the game remind me?

Switch on Study reminders in Settings for a notification when a chapter is learned. You can also add the Panzerkeil widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen to see your commander's progress.

Saves and achievements

Where are my saves?

On your device and, if iCloud sync is on (Settings in the game), in your own iCloud account, so a campaign begun on your iPhone carries on on your iPad. The newest save wins. The game has no accounts of its own.

Where are the achievements?

Tap Awards on the title screen. If you are signed in to Game Center, achievements and the Weekly Operation leaderboard also appear there; anything earned while signed out is sent the next time you sign in.

Accessibility

The text is too small or too large.

Panzerkeil follows the text size set in Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size, and honours Reduce Motion in Settings › Accessibility › Motion. Enemy counters carry a dark pennant, so friend and foe never differ by colour alone.
