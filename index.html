An army that remembers

Name them. Paint them.
Bring them home.

Your units travel with you from battle to battle and grow from Green recruits to Guards. They win medals with lasting bonuses, from the Iron Cross to the Victoria Cross, and earn traits from what they live through.

  • Tank Hunters
  • Street Fighters
  • Winter-Hardened
  • Shell-Shocked
  • Battle-Weary

Every unit keeps a service record. The fallen go on the Roll of Honour. Win a campaign and your army marches in a victory parade.

Morale and the fate of a unit

One blow can
break a line

Units have morale: a surprise against the odds can shake a whole line. Trapped units starve and surrender, and closing a ring is a moment to remember. When a veteran’s life hangs on one blow, the battle slows to watch.

Cheated DeathA veteran that lives through odds that should have destroyed it.
Long ShotA kill against long odds, celebrated on the spot.

Supply, sieges and weather

Fight the war.
And the winter.

Supply

Supply runs along the roads and railheads you hold. Cut the enemy’s lines and their units starve.

Sieges

Surround a city and its defences crumble, turn by turn.

Weather

Snow, mud and storms change how every kind of unit moves and fights, with a three-turn forecast.

Become the commander

From Major to
Field Marshal

Rise over 20 levels. Attributes strengthen the whole army; Command Points buy 13 tactics, from an artillery barrage to an airlift.

Major›Colonel›Brigadier›Major General›Lieutenant General›General›Field Marshal

And while the game is closed, your commander keeps working: studying the Field Manual or training a unit of your Battle Group.
