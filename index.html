The Second World War · 1939 – 1945

Panzerkeil

A turn-based hex wargame for iPhone and iPad. Command the Wehrmacht, the Western Allies or the Red Army across 48 battles.

Common questions

How do I start a different campaign?

On the title screen, tap the campaign you want (Axis, Western Allies or Red Army), then New Campaign or Continue Campaign. Each campaign keeps its own save.

What do the difficulty levels change?

Easy: the enemy fields four-fifths of its strength and makes more mistakes. Normal: the battles as written. Hard: every enemy unit is a grade more experienced, the enemy plays carefully, and victories pay less prestige.

Why did my veteran unit drop a grade?

Refitting with free recruits brings in new men, which lowers a unit's experience. Refitting with veterans costs prestige but keeps the grade. Headquarters warns you before recruits lower a grade.

Where are my saves?

On your device, and, if iCloud sync is on (Settings in the game), in your own iCloud account so a campaign begun on your iPhone carries on on your iPad. The newest save wins. The game has no accounts of its own.

Where are the achievements?

Tap Awards on the title screen. If you are signed in to Game Center (Settings › Game Center), achievements also appear in your Game Center profile; anything earned while signed out is sent the next time you sign in.

The text is too small or too large.

Panzerkeil follows the text size set in Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size, and honours Reduce Motion in Settings › Accessibility › Motion.
