The Invasion of America

A battle that was never fought. In Panzerkeil you can fight it all the same, as the Wehrmacht. Below: what really happened instead, and how the game's version plays.

What happened instead

The invasion of America

This never happened · never fought

No Axis army ever reached the American mainland, though Japan held two Alaskan islands in 1942 and 1943. Germany had no fleet that could have carried an army across the Atlantic, and by 1945 it could not even defend its own cities. Germany surrendered on 8 May 1945, eight weeks before the date of this battle. Twelve days after that date, the first atomic bomb was tested in New Mexico.

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 17 of 17 · 1945

As the Wehrmacht: Unternehmen Amerika

It was never in any plan. But Britain is beaten, Russia has made peace, and the Atlantic belongs to the U-boats; and so, on a summer morning that no staff college ever taught, the landing craft run in on the beaches of the Chesapeake. The Americans have had two years to prepare and have the factories of a continent at their backs. Take the naval base at Norfolk for a harbour, Baltimore and Richmond to hold the flanks, and then the city on the Potomac. It must be done before the armies now crossing the mountains arrive.
