The Second Battle of Kharkov

12 to 28 May 1942. In Panzerkeil you fight it from either side, as the Wehrmacht or as the Red Army. Below: what really happened, and how the game's version plays.

The real battle

The Second Battle of Kharkov

12 to 28 May 1942

On 12 May the Red Army attacked out of the Izyum salient to retake Kharkov. Five days later Kleist's armour struck the base of the salient from the south, under the plan called Fridericus. Stalin refused to halt the offensive until too late. By 22 May the pocket was closed, and the road to Stalingrad and the Caucasus lay open.

The cost. About 277,000 Soviet casualties, some 171,000 of them killed, missing or captured. About 20,000 to 30,000 German casualties.

The result. The Axis won.

Key places

  • Barvenkovo taken by the Axis on 17 May 1942
  • Izyum held by the Allies
  • Lozovaya taken by the Axis on 22 May 1942

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

In Panzerkeil

Fight it yourself

Both sides of this battle are in the game: fight it in each campaign, or in Quick Battle, or against another player in a match, one of you on each side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 7 of 17 · 1942

As the Wehrmacht: Unternehmen Fridericus

Timoshenko has struck first. His armies have burst out of the Izyum bridgehead and are driving west toward Kharkov, deeper into a sack with every mile. The bulge they have made is their own trap. Strike from the north at Balakleya and from the south at Slavyansk, meet on the Donets at Izyum, and cut off everything west of the river. Then clear the pocket: Barvenkovo and Lozovaya. More Soviet armour is coming up east of the Donets, so the door must be shut before it arrives.

The Red Army · stage 4 of 12 · 1942

As the Red Army: Second Kharkov

The front has held through the winter and the Stavka believes the enemy is weak in the south. From the Izyum salient, two shock armies will strike north and west, take Lozovaya and Barvenkovo, and roll on to Kharkov. But the salient has a narrow neck, and the Germans are gathering on its flanks. Move fast. Keep the neck open behind you.
