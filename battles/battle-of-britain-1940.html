The real battle

The Battle of Britain

July to October 1940

The Luftwaffe set out to destroy the Royal Air Force as the first step towards an invasion. Eagle Day, 13 August, opened the main assault. Radar, Fighter Command's control system and Britain's aircraft factories kept the squadrons flying, and on 7 September the bombers turned on London. By the end of October the daylight battle was lost, and the Blitz went on by night.

The cost. About 1,500 RAF aircrew and 2,600 German airmen killed. About 23,000 British civilians died in the bombing by the end of 1940.

The result. The Allies won.

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 3 of 17 · 1940

As the Wehrmacht: Adlerangriff

France has fallen and the army stands on the Channel coast with no way across. Before any invasion the Royal Air Force must be broken: its fighters shot down, the guns that protect its airfields silenced. This is the Luftwaffe's battle, and only the aircraft take part. Escort the bombers, bring Fighter Command to battle over its own fields, and destroy it. Fresh squadrons will come south from the Midlands as the battle goes on, so do not count the enemy beaten until they are.
