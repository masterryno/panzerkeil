Operation Bagration

22 June to 19 August 1944. In Panzerkeil you fight it from either side, as the Wehrmacht or as the Red Army. Below: what really happened, and how the game's version plays.

The real battle

Operation Bagration

22 June to 19 August 1944

On 22 June 1944, three years to the day after the invasion, the Red Army attacked Army Group Centre. Hitler's orders to hold fixed fortress towns left whole corps to be surrounded at Vitebsk and Bobruisk. Minsk was freed on 3 July. In two months the front moved some 500 kilometres west, to the edge of Warsaw.

The cost. About 180,000 Soviet soldiers dead or missing. About 400,000 German casualties. It was the worst defeat in German military history.

The result. The Allies won.

Key places

  • Vitebsk taken by the Allies on 26 June 1944
  • Mogilev taken by the Allies on 28 June 1944
  • Bobruisk taken by the Allies on 29 June 1944
  • Minsk taken by the Allies on 3 July 1944

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

In Panzerkeil

Fight it yourself

Both sides of this battle are in the game: fight it in each campaign, or in Quick Battle, or against another player in a match, one of you on each side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 12 of 17 · 1944

As the Wehrmacht: Bagration

Three years to the day after Barbarossa, the Red Army has come for Army Group Centre. The front in Byelorussia is a great bulge held by infantry in fixed positions; the armour was sent south, where the blow was expected. It is falling here instead, on a scale nobody at headquarters will yet believe. Vitebsk, Mogilev and Bobruisk have been declared fortresses, to be held to the last man. Minsk is the road home for everyone east of it. Hold what can be held, and do not let the fortresses become graves.
