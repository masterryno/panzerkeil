The Battle of Stalingrad

23 August 1942 to 2 February 1943. In Panzerkeil you fight it from either side, as the Wehrmacht or as the Red Army. Below: what really happened, and how the game's version plays.

The real battle

The Battle of Stalingrad

23 August 1942 to 2 February 1943

The Sixth Army reached the Volga on 23 August, the day German bombers set the city ablaze. The fight went on street by street and factory by factory, with the Soviet 62nd Army clinging to a strip along the river. From 19 November two Soviet pincers encircled the attackers. The last of them surrendered on 2 February 1943.

The cost. More than a million Soviet casualties. About 800,000 Axis casualties, with 91,000 taken prisoner, of whom about 6,000 came home. Tens of thousands of civilians died.

The result. The Allies won.

Key places

  • Tractor Works taken by the Axis on 15 October 1942
  • Stalingrad held by the Allies
  • Beketovka held by the Allies

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

In Panzerkeil

Fight it yourself

Both sides of this battle are in the game: fight it in each campaign, or in Quick Battle, or against another player in a match, one of you on each side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 8 of 17 · 1942

As the Wehrmacht: Stalingrad

The Don is behind you and the Volga is sixty kilometres ahead across open steppe. Sixth Army is to take Stalingrad and cut the river. The approach is a matter of days: break the line at Kalach, overrun the airfield at Gumrak. The city is another matter. Its three districts, the Tractor Works in the north, the centre, and Beketovka in the south, are being fortified street by street, and every night the ferries bring fresh divisions across from the east bank. Take it quickly or it will take the army.
