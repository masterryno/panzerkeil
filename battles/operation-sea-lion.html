Operation Sea Lion

A battle that was never fought. In Panzerkeil you can fight it all the same, as the Wehrmacht. Below: what really happened instead, and how the game's version plays.

What happened instead

The invasion of Britain

Planned for September 1940 · never fought

No German army crossed the Channel. The Luftwaffe failed to win control of the air over Britain, and on 17 September 1940 Hitler postponed the invasion indefinitely. The invasion barges were dispersed, and German planning turned east, towards the Soviet Union.

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 4 of 17 · 1940

As the Wehrmacht: Unternehmen Seelöwe

Fighter Command is broken and the barges have crossed in the night. The first wave is ashore between Brighton and Hastings with what it could carry: infantry, a handful of tanks, few guns. Everything depends on the ports. Take Brighton, Portsmouth and Dover so that the follow-up divisions and their armour can land, then break the stop-line south of the Thames and take London. The British have little left, but what they have is coming south as fast as it can march.
