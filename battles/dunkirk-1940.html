The evacuation of Dunkirk, 26 May to 4 June 1940. In Panzerkeil you fight it as the Western Allies. Below: what really happened, and how the game's version plays.

The real battle

The evacuation of Dunkirk

26 May to 4 June 1940

With the British Expeditionary Force and French armies trapped against the Channel, the Royal Navy began Operation Dynamo on 26 May. A German halt order and a stubborn rearguard bought time. Destroyers, ferries and hundreds of small civilian boats lifted about 338,000 men from the harbour mole and the beaches before Dunkirk fell on 4 June.

The cost. About 240 vessels sunk. About 40,000 French troops, many of them from the rearguard, were captured when the town fell.

The result. The Allies won.

Key places

  • Cassel taken by the Axis on 30 May 1940
  • Furnes taken by the Axis on 1 June 1940
  • Bergues taken by the Axis on 2 June 1940
  • Dunkirk taken by the Axis on 4 June 1940

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

The Western Allies · stage 1 of 13 · 1940

As the Western Allies: Operation Dynamo

The panzers have reached the Channel and the British Expeditionary Force is cut off in Flanders with its back to the sea. The army is to be taken off the beaches at Dunkirk, and every hour the perimeter holds is another division saved. Hold the port, the canal line at Bergues and Furnes, and the hill town of Cassel to the south for as long as you can. Ground can be given up; the army cannot.
