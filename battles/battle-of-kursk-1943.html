The Battle of Kursk

Operation Citadel, 5 to 16 July 1943. In Panzerkeil you fight it from either side, as the Wehrmacht or as the Red Army. Below: what really happened, and how the game's version plays.

The real battle

Operation Citadel

5 to 16 July 1943

Operation Citadel attacked both sides of the Kursk bulge on 5 July 1943, into some of the deepest defences ever built. The northern pincer stalled within days. In the south, the armour came close to Prokhorovka, where a vast tank battle was fought on 12 July. With the Allies landing in Sicily and the Red Army striking elsewhere, Hitler called it off.

The cost. About 54,000 German and 178,000 Soviet casualties. It was the last German strategic offensive in the east.

The result. The Allies won.

Key places

  • Prokhorovka held by the Allies
  • Oboyan held by the Allies
  • Kursk held by the Allies

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

In Panzerkeil

Fight it yourself

Both sides of this battle are in the game: fight it in each campaign, or in Quick Battle, or against another player in a match, one of you on each side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 10 of 17 · 1943

As the Wehrmacht: Unternehmen Zitadelle

The last great offensive in the east. The Kursk salient is to be cut off at its base, and this is the southern arm of the pincer. Nothing about it is a surprise: the Red Army has had three months to dig, and three belts of trenches, minefields and anti-tank guns lie between Belgorod and Oboyan. Behind them wait the tank armies. Break the belts, take Oboyan on the Kursk road, and meet their armour at Prokhorovka. Kursk itself is the prize if anything is left to reach it with.

The Red Army · stage 8 of 12 · 1943

As the Red Army: The Kursk Bulge

For three months the Germans have prepared to pinch off the Kursk salient, and for three months we have known it. Three belts of trenches, minefields and anti-tank guns lie across their path, and the tank armies wait behind them. Let them break themselves on the defences, then meet them at Prokhorovka. Hold Oboyan, Prokhorovka and Kursk.
