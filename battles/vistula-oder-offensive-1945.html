The Vistula–Oder Offensive

The Vistula-Oder Offensive, 12 January to 2 February 1945. In Panzerkeil you fight it from either side, as the Wehrmacht or as the Red Army. Below: what really happened, and how the game's version plays.

The real battle

The Vistula-Oder Offensive

12 January to 2 February 1945

From bridgeheads on the Vistula, the Red Army attacked on 12 January 1945 with an overwhelming advantage in men, tanks and guns. Warsaw, by then a ruined city, was freed on 17 January. Soviet troops reached Auschwitz on 27 January. By early February they were on the Oder, some 70 kilometres from Berlin. Fortress Poznan held out until 23 February.

The cost. About 194,000 Soviet casualties. Hundreds of thousands of German casualties and prisoners, while millions of German civilians fled west.

The result. The Allies won.

Key places

  • Warsaw taken by the Allies on 17 January 1945
  • Krakow taken by the Allies on 19 January 1945
  • Lodz taken by the Allies on 19 January 1945
  • Poznan taken by the Allies on 23 February 1945

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

In Panzerkeil

Fight it yourself

Both sides of this battle are in the game: fight it in each campaign, or in Quick Battle, or against another player in a match, one of you on each side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 15 of 17 · 1945

As the Wehrmacht: Die Weichsel

The front has stood on the Vistula since the summer, and the Red Army has used every week of it. Opposite the bridgeheads at Magnuszew and Sandomierz it has massed five men for every one of ours, five tanks, seven guns. The armour that might have met them went to the Ardennes. When the barrage lifts, what is left of the line must hold long enough for the reserves to form behind it. Warsaw, Lodz, Krakow, Poznan: every one that stands is a week bought for the Oder.

The Red Army · stage 11 of 12 · 1945

As the Red Army: The Vistula–Oder Offensive
