The Balkans Campaign

The invasion of Yugoslavia and Greece, 6 to 30 April 1941. In Panzerkeil you fight it as the Wehrmacht. Below: what really happened, and how the game's version plays.

The real battle

The invasion of Yugoslavia and Greece

6 to 30 April 1941

Germany attacked Yugoslavia and Greece on 6 April 1941, bombing Belgrade on the first morning. Yugoslavia surrendered on 17 April. In Greece, German armour went through the mountain passes and around the Metaxas Line, forcing British and Commonwealth troops into retreat. Athens fell on 27 April, and most of the British force was evacuated by sea.

The cost. About 1,300 German dead. About 13,000 Greek dead since the Italian invasion of October 1940. Thousands of civilians were killed in the bombing of Belgrade.

The result. The Axis won.

Key places

  • Salonika taken by the Axis on 9 April 1941
  • Belgrade taken by the Axis on 12 April 1941
  • Sarajevo taken by the Axis on 15 April 1941
  • Athens taken by the Axis on 27 April 1941

In the game, when the battle ends, your result is set beside this one: who held each town and when, the account and the cost, read aloud by the general of your side.

The Axis Campaign · stage 3 of 17 · 1941

As the Wehrmacht: Unternehmen Marita

A coup in Belgrade has torn up Yugoslavia's pact with the Axis, and the British have landed in Greece. Both problems are to be solved at once. Take Belgrade and Sarajevo to finish Yugoslavia, then drive down the Vardar to Salonika and on to Athens. The mountains belong to the infantry; the armour must keep to the roads and the valleys.
